title: Low Tide picture logic

Picture-logic puzzles from the shore: **12 levels** from a 5 × 5 warm-up to 10 × 10, plus a new puzzle every day under **Today**.

Fill cells so each row and column shows its runs of filled cells in order, and a picture appears; the bulb explains the next step.

Every puzzle has exactly one solution and needs no guessing; your marks are kept, so you can stop and come back.
