Saturday 12 December · one day

Tram up, Alfama down, Belém for the sunset

Tram 28E lifts you to the top of the old town before the castle opens; the morning is all downhill on foot. After lunch the 15E runs along the river to Belém for two pastéis, the monastery and the tower at sunset, then back to Rossio for a ginja and dinner.

Castelo de São Jorge

Buy ahead · not timed

The castle crowns the hill above Alfama. Walk the walls and towers for the long view over the Baixa's grid of streets to the river, and arrive early, while the courtyards are still quiet.

Mosteiro dos Jerónimos

Timed entry · book 15:00

King Manuel I's monastery for the Order of Saint Jerome, begun in 1501–02. The two-storey cloister, the high point of the carved Manueline style, is what the ticket buys; the church is free.

Open
Cloister Tuesday–Sunday 09:30–17:30, last entry 17:00. Church on Saturdays 10:30–17:00. Ticket kiosk closes 16:30.
Ticket
18 €; half price for 65+ and ages 13–24; 12 and under free. Buy online: the monastery says tickets often sell out and cannot then be bought on the day.

TipDo the cloister first, then the church, so you leave on time for the 16:30 tower entry.

16:30 – 17:30 · sunset 17:15

Torre de Belém

Timed entry · book 16:30

Built 1514–1519 on a rock in the river to guard the way into Lisbon. It reopened in late May 2026 after a year of restoration, now with entry in 30-minute slots and about 900 visitors a day. Enter at 16:30 and you can still be on the top terrace when the sun goes down over the river mouth, to the west-southwest, at 17:15; the tower closes at 17:30.

Open
Tuesday–Sunday 09:30–17:30, last entry 17:00. May close in bad weather, with a refund.
Ticket
15 €; half price for 65+ and ages 13–24; 12 and under free
Light
Sunset 17:15, civil dusk 17:45

TipOnly the ground floor of the bastion is step-free; the rest is narrow stone stairs. If the tower is shut, the riverside lawn faces the same sunset.

18:20 – 19:25 · ginja and market

A Ginjinha and the Rossio market

Market free · ginja at the counter

A shot of ginja, Lisbon's sour-cherry liqueur, from the hole-in-the-wall bar that has poured it since about 1840. You drink it standing in Largo de São Domingos; ask for it com or sem, with or without the cherry. Round the corner, Rossio's Christmas market fills the square with craft and food stalls, and a free electric Christmas train loops through the Baixa.

If it rains, or something shuts

  • Rain in the morning: the castle is open-air, so keep it short and swap the Alfama wander for the Museu do Fado at morning stop 3 (10:00–18:00, 5 €).
  • Rain in Belém: Pastéis de Belém, the Jerónimos church and the cloister's arcaded walks keep you dry. The Torre may close in bad weather and refunds your ticket; spend the time in the Jerónimos church instead (Saturday until 17:00).
  • Tram diverted: Carris posted changes to the 28E, 12E and 15E in November 2026; check its service-changes page that morning. If the 15E is out, the CP train on the Cascais line runs from Cais do Sodré to Belém (separate fare, not on the 24-hour Carris/Metro ticket). Carris service changes

Before you go

Getting around

  • Ticket: buy a navegante occasional card (0,50 €; it replaced the Viva Viagem card, one card per person) at a Metro machine and load the 24-hour Carris/Metro ticket (7,25 €). It starts at your first tap at 08:23 and covers every tram, bus and Metro ride until the next morning.
  • Other fares: Zapping pay-as-you-go is 1,72 € a Carris ride (loaded in steps of 3, 5, 10 € and up), so it only beats the day ticket if you ride four times or fewer. Paying on board costs 3,30 € a tram ride, by contactless card at the validator; the articulated trams on the 15E take no cash.
  • Boarding: tap the card on the validator every time you get on.
  • Lines today: 28E Martim Moniz – Campo de Ourique (Prazeres), 15E Praça da Figueira – Algés. Colours as on Carris's own timetables.
  • Emergency: 112.
  • Tipping: optional. Round up at cafés and bars; at a sit-down restaurant check the bill for serviço first, and if there is none, up to 10 % in cash.
