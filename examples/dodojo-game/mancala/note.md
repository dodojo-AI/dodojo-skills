title: Mancala

Mancala with the Kalah rules, 6 pits a side and 4 seeds in each: play the computer (Easy, Medium or Hard) or a friend, on one phone or each on your own in a team space. Tap a pit on your side to sow its seeds counterclockwise.

Last seed in your store: go again. Last seed in an empty pit of yours: capture the seeds opposite. Most seeds in your store wins.
