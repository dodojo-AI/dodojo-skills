title: Gomoku

Gomoku on a 15 × 15 board: play the computer (Easy, Medium or Hard) or a friend on the same device. Black moves first, five or more in a row wins with no forbidden moves, and the players swap colors after every game.

Every move is kept in this Note, so a game left halfway carries on when you open it again, on any of your devices. Your record and fastest wins are kept here too.
