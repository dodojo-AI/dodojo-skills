Rules
  • Black moves first, then the players take turns placing one stone on an empty point. Stones never move.
  • Five in a row across, down or diagonally wins, and so does six or more: this page plays freestyle gomoku, with no forbidden moves.
  • If the board fills up without a five, the game is a draw.
  • Moving first is a real advantage, so the players swap colors after every game.
  • Against the computer you can undo as often as you like, back to before your last move. Between two players, each may take back their own last move once a game.
  • Close the Note halfway through and the game waits here for you, on any of your devices.
Terms
Open three
Three in a row with both ends open. One more stone makes an open four, so it has to be blocked at once.
Four
One stone short of five with one end already blocked. Blocking the other end stops it.
Open four
Four in a row with both ends open. Only one end can be blocked, so it becomes five on the next move.
Four-three
One move that makes a four and an open three at once. Block the four and the three goes through: almost always a win.
Forbidden moves
Tournament renju forbids Black double threes, double fours and lines of six or more, to offset the advantage of moving first. This page leaves them out so the game is easy to pick up.
