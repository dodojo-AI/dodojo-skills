title: Number Bomb

A party game for 2–10 people around one phone: the box hides a bomb number between 1 and 100, everyone takes turns calling a number, and each miss shrinks the range toward the bomb. **Whoever hits the bomb loses** and starts the next round.

It opens ready to play: set the number of players under the game; names, the range (1–50 / 1–1000) and forfeits are in the settings below. Supports English / 中文 / 日本語, follows your device's language, and can be switched on the page.
