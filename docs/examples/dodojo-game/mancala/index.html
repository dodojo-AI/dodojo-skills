How to play · Kalah, 6 pits × 4 seeds

Tap a pit on your side to sow its seeds counterclockwise, one per pit. Last seed in your store: go again. Last seed in an empty pit of yours: capture the pit opposite. Most seeds in your store wins.

    Full rules: Kalah (6 pits, 4 seeds)
    1. The board has six pits on each side and a store at each end. Your pits are the row nearest you; your store is on your right.
    2. Each pit starts with 4 seeds, 48 in all. Who moves first alternates from game to game.
    3. On your turn, pick up every seed in one of your pits and sow them counterclockwise, one per pit, into your own store but never your opponent's.
    4. If your last seed lands in your store, you take another turn.
    5. If your last seed lands in an empty pit on your side and the pit opposite holds seeds, you capture both: that seed and the seeds opposite go to your store. (Some sets capture even when the opposite pit is empty; these rules do not.)
    6. The game ends as soon as either row is empty. Each player then puts the seeds left on their own side into their own store.
    7. Most seeds wins; 24–24 is a draw.
    8. Take-backs: against the computer as many as you like, and they are counted; between two people, one each per game, before the other player moves.
