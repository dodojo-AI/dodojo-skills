How to play

Everyone gets a secret word. One or two of you get a word that is almost the same, and nobody knows who.

Go round describing your word in one short line, then vote out the player you think has the odd word.

Catch every odd one and the civilians win; survive until you are as many as them and the odd side wins.

Full rules
  1. Players: 3 to 10. With 3 to 6 players one person gets the odd word; with 7 to 10, two do. From 6 players you can add a blank, who gets no word at all (on by default from 8).
  2. The deal: pass the phone round. Each player holds the button, reads their word, lets go and passes on. Nobody knows whether their own word is the odd one; only the blank knows they have none.
  3. Describing: the named player starts and it goes round the table. Each says one short line about their word: never the word itself, part of it, a rhyme or a translation. Be vague enough to hide and clear enough to be trusted.
  4. Voting by passing the phone: each player opens their ballot alone and picks one name; nobody votes for themselves. Voting by a show of hands: on three everyone points, and the host taps each name once per hand.
  5. The player with the most votes goes out and their envelope is opened: civilian, odd one or blank. Their word stays secret.
  6. A tie: the tied players each say one more line, then everyone votes again between them only. Tied again: nobody goes out and a new round of describing begins.
  7. Winning: the civilians win when every odd one and the blank are out. The odd side wins as soon as they are as many as the civilians still in.
  8. Scoring: when the civilians win, every civilian scores 1, in or out. When the odd side wins, every odd one and the blank score 3. After the last deal the most points wins the evening; ties share the place.
  9. Breaking the rules: say your word aloud and the table votes you out that round. If someone sees another player's word, end the deal under Settings and deal again.
  10. Fair turns: each deal starts one seat further round the table, and a random player describes first.
